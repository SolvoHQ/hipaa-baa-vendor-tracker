## Money questions (quotable answers)

### Which AI tools sign a HIPAA BAA?
Of the 34 AI tools tracked, **1** signs a HIPAA BAA on a generally-available basis (Google Gemini). A further **9** sign one only on a qualifying/enterprise tier (ChatGPT, Claude, Codeium / Windsurf, ElevenLabs, Grammarly, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Otter.ai, Slack AI, Notion AI), and **3** sign one on request through sales (Fireflies.ai, Grok, Tabnine). The remaining **21** do not sign a BAA on any plan, or explicitly carve their AI feature out of an existing BAA. Every verdict below links a dated primary source.

### Which AI tools are HIPAA-eligible (can process PHI)?
An AI tool is HIPAA-eligible **only** when the vendor has signed a BAA that covers your specific plan, and only on that covered tier. Today that means: **Gemini for Workspace** (BAA-covered Workspace), and — on a qualifying enterprise tier or by request — **ChatGPT, Claude, Codeium / Windsurf, ElevenLabs, Grammarly, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Otter.ai, Slack AI, Notion AI, Fireflies.ai, Grok, Tabnine**. On consumer and free tiers, none of these are PHI-eligible. Every other tool in this dataset is **not** HIPAA-eligible: do not enter PHI.

### Can I put PHI (protected health information) into an AI chatbot?
Only when the vendor has signed a HIPAA BAA that covers your specific plan, and only on that covered tier. On most tools a BAA is enterprise-gated or unavailable, so **consumer and free tiers are never PHI-eligible**. Confirm a current, executed BAA directly with the vendor before sending any PHI. This dataset is cited public information, **not legal advice**.

### What does "BAA on enterprise tiers" mean for HIPAA?
It means the vendor will sign a BAA, but only on a specific (usually enterprise or business) plan — the free and consumer tiers are contractually excluded and cannot lawfully process PHI. The "How to get a BAA" column names the exact tier each tool gates its BAA behind.

### Is any AI tool "HIPAA compliant"?
No AI tool is "HIPAA compliant" or "HIPAA certified" as a standalone property — there is no such certification, and the term is a shared responsibility between you and the vendor. An AI tool can only be *used in a HIPAA-compliant way* once the vendor has signed a HIPAA BAA covering your specific plan, and only on that covered tier. So "Is ChatGPT HIPAA compliant?" really means "will it sign a BAA, and on which tier?" — which is exactly what this dataset tracks. Each per-vendor answer below leads with that verdict.

## Status legend

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| ✅ Signs a BAA | Vendor offers a HIPAA BAA on a generally-available basis. |
| 🟡 BAA on enterprise/qualifying tier only | A BAA exists, but only on an enterprise / qualifying plan (often with seat or tier minimums). |
| 🟡 BAA on request | A BAA is available through sales / a questionnaire, not self-serve. |
| ❌ Will not sign / prohibits PHI | No BAA on any tier, or the product is explicitly carved out of the vendor's BAA. |
| ❓ Unverified | No sourced verdict yet — we show this rather than guess. |

**PHI-eligible** is *Conditional* when PHI is allowed only under specific conditions (a signed BAA, a particular tier, or a ZDR-enabled API) — read the tier detail and the source before relying on it.

## Methodology

- Every verdict is derived from the vendor's **own** BAA, DPA, HIPAA, or compliance documentation (linked in the Source column), or from HHS — not from third-party summaries where a primary source exists.
- Each row is **date-stamped** (`Last verified`). A verdict is only as current as that date — vendors change BAA scope, so re-check the source for anything high-stakes.
- An **unverified** entry never outranks a sourced verdict; we would rather show `❓ Unverified` than guess (a YMYL guardrail for health data).
- A BAA covering a vendor's core product does **not** automatically cover its AI add-on. Several tools here are ❌ for exactly that reason (the AI feature is carved out of an otherwise HIPAA-ready product).
- **Scope:** this dataset tracks the HIPAA BAA + PHI question only. We deliberately do **not** assert SOC 2 type, ISO 27001, or other certifications here — we have not verified those to a primary source for every row, and a YMYL health reference should not carry uncited "mentions SOC2"-grade claims. Where a vendor's trust center documents SOC 2, it is linked from that vendor's [BAA Atlas](https://baa-atlas.vercel.app/ai/hipaa) detail page rather than asserted here.
- This is a documentation reference, **not legal advice**. Confirm coverage in your own executed agreement before processing PHI.
